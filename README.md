# Mumbai Regional Conference — Official Website

The official website of the Mumbai Regional Conference (MRC), Methodist Church in India. The site presents the heritage, leadership, churches, and educational institutions of the region to its members and the wider public.

## Pages

| Page | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Welcome hero, key statistics, introduction to the conference |
| **About** | Heritage and history of the Mumbai Regional Conference |
| **Churches** | Searchable directory of churches in the region, including districts and map links |
| **Leadership** | Episcopal succession and governance of the conference |
| **Education** | Educational institutions run under the conference |

## Content

- **Heritage** — The legacy of Methodism in Mumbai and the story of the conference.
- **Churches** — A directory of parish and memorial churches across the region with details for each congregation.
- **Leadership** — The office of the Bishop, the episcopal succession, and the governing bodies of the conference.
- **Education** — Schools and institutions nurtured under the conference's mission.
