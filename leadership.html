Our Resident Bishop

Bishop Dr. Newton M. Parmar

Episcopal and Resident Bishop, Mumbai Regional Conference

Bishop Dr. Newton M. Parmar currently serves as the Episcopal and Resident Bishop of the Mumbai Regional Conference of the Methodist Church in India, leading the conference under the banner of MRC-MCI.

Before his election to the episcopacy, Newton Parmar was deeply involved in national-level MCI administration as the Secretary of the Executive Board of the Methodist Church in India. He has been a director of the Methodist Church in India Trust Association since 2018.

His institutional footprint includes serving as Chairman of Hutchings School Talegaon and holding leadership roles with Ingraham Institute, Ghaziabad — a ministry that extends across the national MCI structure.

As the shepherd of MRC, Bishop Parmar leads the 38th annual session of the conference and the wider network of districts, pastorates, and institutions in Mumbai, Pune, and beyond.

How We Are Organized

Governance Structure

The Methodist Church in India is organized hierarchically. Understanding the structure helps understand how MRC functions:

  • General Conference
    The highest periodic representative governing body.
  • Council of Bishops
    Episcopal leadership of the national church.
  • Regional Conference (MRC)
    Regional governance — the Mumbai Regional Conference.
  • District
    Administered by District Superintendents.
  • Pastorate Conference
    Local church governance — the Pastorate Committee is its administrative body.
  • Local Ministry Organizations
    MYF (youth), WSCS (women), Methodist Men, Sunday School.
